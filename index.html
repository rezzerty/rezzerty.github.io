A clearer view of your veterans' evidence.

CaseVac Ai brings the existing Command Center, VA Desk, dashboard and scanner work together under one application name. Its purpose is to help organize records, follow claim history and review evidence with its sources.

Current access: private owner workspace

What is available today

The private interface contains a reviewed case-reference snapshot and links to original sources. Its manual Google Drive reader is configured for two project Google Docs. It requests read-only Google permission and reports a separate result for each document.

The owner has verified successful reads of both configured project documents. Each later refresh still has its own result and timestamp. Refreshing a source does not automatically update the reviewed case snapshot or establish a new VA claim status.

One CaseVac Ai system, with clear limits

VA Desk and Command Center

Existing navigation, case-reference screens and source links belong to the same CaseVac Ai project. Their original records and source names remain in place.

Dashboard and scanner

The existing dashboard and scanner are preserved project components. This public site does not start scans, upload documents or provide an always-running evidence review.

ChatGPT integration

The private app publishes an authenticated ChatGPT MCP connection, and its status tool has responded successfully. The connection uses selected synced source copies with dated coverage information; this check does not establish that every tool or document works. This public website does not accept evidence uploads or run automated evaluation.

Keep originals at the center of the work

Use source links and read-status details to distinguish saved information from a fresh document read. CaseVac Ai does not submit claims, sign documents or verify entitlement for you.

For help with the existing private connection, visit Support. Please keep medical records, claim numbers and credentials out of public posts and initial support emails.
